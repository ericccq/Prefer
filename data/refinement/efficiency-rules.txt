# Crazy Eights — Shared Random Set-aside
Author-clarified efficiency rules, revision shared_random_suit_v2, 2026-10-13.

## 1. Game State
Four players have private hands. The stock is hidden; its size is public.
The starter pile, active suit, hand sizes, shared random set-aside suit, and
each player's set-aside area and accumulated points are public. Set-aside
cards stay outside the hand and stock.

## 2. Cards
Use a standard 52-card deck without jokers. Eights are wild when played from
a hand. Card values: eight 50; K/Q/J/10 10; ace 1; others their pip value.

## 3. Setup
Deal five cards to each player in round-robin order. Reveal a non-eight starter;
bury any starter eight in the middle of the stock and reveal the next card.
Randomly select exactly one of the four suits for the entire table. All four
players share this set-aside suit; no player chooses a personal suit.
Immediately move every hand card of this suit into its owner's face-up
set-aside area, including eights. Add each card's normal point value to that
owner's set-aside points. This is automatic setup, not a player action or turn.
These cards do not change the starter or declare a suit. The shared suit stays
fixed. The seeded browser demo makes the random suit reproducible for a deal.

## 4. Legal Actions
Play one card matching the active suit or the starter's rank. An eight may be
played regardless of the match; declare one of the OTHER THREE suits. The
shared set-aside suit is forbidden for suit declaration. You may draw one card,
even with a playable card. A playable draw may be kept; drawing may continue.
Any drawn card of the shared random suit goes directly into its owner's area,
including eights. It does not enter your hand, change the starter, declare a
suit, or end your turn; add its normal card value to the owner's set-aside
points. No extra action is required to place or score it. Pass only with an
empty stock and no playable hand card.

## 5. Turn Resolution
Playing a hand card ends your turn. Drawing keeps the turn with you. A draw
of the shared random suit is revealed and moved directly to your set-aside area.
Other draws enter your private hand. Turns proceed through Players 0,1,2,3.

## 6. Set-aside Area
Set-aside cards never return to the stock or hand and cannot be played. They
do not count toward hand size or remaining-hand value; they do contribute
their normal card values to their owner's separate set-aside score. If setup empties any
hands, those players share an immediate win. Emptying your remaining hand
during play wins immediately.

## 7. Information
Your hand and ordinary stock draws are private. Set-aside cards are public.
Opponent hands stay hidden. Revealing a set-aside card exposes that card only.

## 8. Payoffs
With an empty stock, a full cycle of passes ends the game; the lowest remaining
hand value wins. Tied winners share the win. Each loser pays their remaining
hand value, and winners split the total. Each winner's collected payoff is
then doubled. Add each player's set-aside points to their own game payoff,
including losing players. The winner's set-aside score is not doubled: their
final score is set-aside points plus twice the original collected payoff.
Losers' final scores are their set-aside points minus their hand-value payment.
The extra winner bonus does not increase the losers' individual payments.
